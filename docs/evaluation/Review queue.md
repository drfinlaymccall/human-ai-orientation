# Review queue

Start here rather than copying a template. These are prepared tasks, not completed evaluations. No deadlines or review dates have been assigned.

- [ ] **First:** [[Page review - 01 Computer use and context management]] — open the page and check the introduction to files, notes and AI basics.
- [ ] **Next:** [[Page review - 02 A home for your notes]] — try the notes setup and context-reuse instructions.

## The short version

1. Open a review note and click its page link.
2. Insert the date beside **Started** when you begin (see [[Obsidian quick setup]]).
3. Type or dictate what works, what gets in the way and what matters most. Headings are prompts, not compulsory fields.
4. Tick **Ready for triage** inside the note; tick the queue task when you have handed that review over. Neither checkbox means a fix is implemented or accepted.
5. Wait for sync, then ask the VPS agent to process the ready evaluations.

The agent handles criterion mapping, finding IDs, proposed fixes, acceptance checks and register updates. You retain approval of actions and final owner acceptance. The full [[Evaluation framework]] is available for reference, not required reading before every review.
