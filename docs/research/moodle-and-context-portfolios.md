## Resulting design position

Keep the three-activity MVP, add an explicit portfolio-building bridge, and assess success through retrieval and reuse in a fresh task. Complete useful-task and check/continue content before optional capability expansion. Collect real pilot evidence before deciding the next three improvements. Keep text/manual/example routes available and separate private reflection from shareable context.

## Email setup sources

[Google app-password guidance](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833?hl=en) explains the 2-Step Verification requirement, account restrictions and revocation behaviour. Google prefers OAuth where supported; the current small pilot uses a dedicated app password entered directly into Moodle's protected admin form. OAuth/provider migration is an operating decision if the pilot expands. Local Moodle 4.5 source (`admin/settings/server.php`, `lib/moodlelib.php`, `lib/phpmailer/moodle_phpmailer.php`) was inspected for SMTP host, TLS, LOGIN and email handling. SMTP acceptance and owner-confirmed receipt are recorded separately from automated capture tests.
