# Copy/paste and app-switching support

Checked 13 October 2026 using the publishers’ pages. Optional support appears in preparation and the try/check/improve activity; no resource is copied or embedded.

| Source | Use and limits |
| --- | --- |
| [Apple Mac keyboard shortcuts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102650) | Command+A/C/V and Command+Tab; learner-facing instructions also offer menus and clicking browser tabs. |
| [Microsoft keyboard shortcuts](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/windows/keyboard-shortcuts-in-windows) | Ctrl+A/C/V and Alt+Tab; app-specific behaviour can vary. |
| [Be Connected: What is a computer?](https://beconnected.esafety.gov.au/topic-library/computer-basics-for-beginners/what-is-a-computer) | Guest-readable course with introductory video and written transcript, plus screen, keyboard and mouse activities. Signing in saves progress. The video introduces computers; do not claim it demonstrates our AI workflow. |
| [Be Connected: Type text into a text field](https://beconnected.esafety.gov.au/topic-library/practice-area/practice-area-for-mouse-and-keyboard/type-text-into-a-text-field) | Guest-access mouse/keyboard practice. Interactive activity is labelled desktop/laptop only. Our written phone/tablet route remains available. |

The in-course guide explains selecting text, the clipboard, focusing the destination, pasting, switching tabs/apps and returning with an answer. It includes a short notes-app practice and an undo/troubleshooting route. Phone instructions are intentionally general because selection menus vary by device and app.

Links and published content were inspected; native Mac/Windows/phone controls and complete external activities were not executed. A short captioned course-specific demonstration remains a useful later addition. Review links and guest access during weekly maintenance.
