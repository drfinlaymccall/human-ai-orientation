## T0: introduce the course

I made a short course for people who want to do something useful with AI and would like a clearer place to start.

Human AI Orientation begins with a task that matters to you. You try it, give the AI better context, check what comes back and keep something useful for next time: a small personal context portfolio and a next-step plan.

Bring your own AI tool. A free account is enough, and fictional examples let you explore without sharing private information. The three activities and portfolio practice take roughly 70–95 minutes, at your own pace. Files-and-notes preparation is there if you need it.

Try the course: https://orientation.finlaymccall.com/

## T0 + 3 days: try one useful task

What would make an AI result useful to you?

That is where the first activity in Human AI Orientation starts. Choose a small task and one sign of success before asking for a result. Then compare what you receive with what you actually needed.

For an invitation, that might mean a friendly paragraph that leaves an undecided date blank rather than inventing it. Your task can be completely different; the useful habit is deciding what to check.

Try the activity: https://orientation.finlaymccall.com/first-task

## T0 + 7 days: give the right background

A clearer request starts with deciding what background matters.

The context activity in Human AI Orientation helps you build a short brief: the task, audience, useful information, boundaries and a way to judge the result. You try it, inspect what changed and decide what to keep or correct.

You also practise leaving things out. Your private history and every file you own are rarely what a small task needs.

Build and test a brief: https://orientation.finlaymccall.com/preview

## T0 + 10 days: save context for next time

Useful context is easier to reuse when you can find it again.

The portfolio practice in Human AI Orientation brings together three short documents: how you like to work, the context for a goal or project, and useful knowledge or examples.

Review the content, remove guesses and download your own copy. You can select the relevant parts for a fresh conversation without assuming the AI remembers an earlier chat. The restore backup stays with you; a course login does not store your private portfolio.

Build your context portfolio: https://orientation.finlaymccall.com/portfolio

## T0 + 14 days: check and choose what comes next

A fluent answer still needs your judgement.

The final activity in Human AI Orientation asks you to reuse selected context, check a result, identify an improvement and choose a worthwhile next goal. What do you need to learn? What background would the AI need? What is one manageable first step?

The aim is practical experience you can explain and use again. Completion records practice; it is not a certificate of AI expertise.

Try the activity: https://orientation.finlaymccall.com/check-and-continue
