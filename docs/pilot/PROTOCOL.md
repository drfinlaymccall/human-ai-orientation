# Small human pilot

Owner: Finlay. Target: 3–5 willing participants. Status: prepared; no participants recruited by the agent and no observations fabricated.

## Invitation draft — Finlay sends when ready

I'm trying a short course that helps you do something useful with AI and keep context you can reuse. Would you be willing to try it and tell me where it helps or gets in the way? You can use fictional details and stop at any time. You don't need a paid AI plan. I'd particularly like to see whether you can save your context, return later and use it for a fresh task. Your personal portfolio can stay on your device. I'll ask separately before quoting any feedback publicly.

## Session

1. Ask what they hope to do, their device, prior AI experience, and any access preferences. Do not label confidence beforehand.
2. Ask them to find an appropriate starting point without coaching navigation.
3. Let them choose a small task and success criterion, try it, improve context and inspect the result.
4. Build a short portfolio. Ask them to explain one included and one omitted detail.
5. Download Markdown and backup; close the browser; return and restore. Record assistance needed without retaining the portfolio.
6. Start a fresh AI conversation with selected context. Ask them to judge relevance, inaccuracies and what to change.
7. Ask which next goal matters, what they need to learn, what the AI needs, and whether they would use this again.

## Private observation template

Participant code; consent/date; device; starting goal; where help was needed; save/reopen outcome; context-selection explanation; checked result; perceived usefulness; approximate time; access barriers; suggested change; permission for any public quote. Store completed forms under ignored `trial/pilot/`. No real names/emails in public issues. Do not record passwords, AI chats or personal files without explicit need and consent.

## Public synthesis template

Number invited/participated/completed; what was observed; evidence limitations; recurring friction; decision; at most three next improvements; corresponding issue/commit; next check. Separate human findings, Finlay's own walkthrough and synthetic browser findings. Do not infer learning gain from page views or completion ticks.
