# Small human pilot

Owner: Finlay. Target: 3–5 willing participants. Status: prepared; no participants recruited by the agent and no observations fabricated.

## Invitation draft — Finlay sends when ready

I'm trying a short course that helps you do something useful with AI and keep context you can reuse. Would you be willing to try it and tell me where it helps or gets in the way? You can use fictional details and stop at any time. You don't need a paid AI plan. I'd particularly like to see whether you can save your context, return later and use it for a fresh task. Your personal portfolio can stay on your device. I'll ask separately before quoting any feedback publicly.

## Session

1. Ask what they hope to do, their device, prior AI experience, and any access preferences. Do not label confidence beforehand.
2. Start at the homepage without coaching navigation. Ask what they expect to do and keep, then let them choose a starting point from the activity map or primary action. Record whether optional preparation appears compulsory.
3. Use their consenting email address for the real registration flow: create the account, find confirmation mail (including phone/app switching), confirm, self-enrol and reach the chosen activity. Check missing-mail help if needed; do not collect their password or confirmation link. Record delivery/assistance separately from the earlier synthetic captured-mail results.
4. Let them choose a small task and success criterion, try it, improve context and inspect the result.
5. Build a short portfolio. Ask them to explain one included and one omitted detail.
6. Download Markdown and backup; close the browser; return and restore, using another device if available. Record assistance needed without retaining the portfolio.
7. Mark a manual practice activity done, sign out and sign in in a fresh browser session. Ask them to find their actual saved progress and continue; distinguish this from restoring the portfolio. Check that completed activities are still revisitable. A Lesson interruption should offer its native resume route.
8. Start a fresh AI conversation with selected context. Ask them to judge relevance, inaccuracies and what to change.
9. Ask which next goal matters, what they need to learn, what the AI needs, and whether they would use this again.

## Private observation template

Participant code; consent/date; device/access preferences; starting goal; homepage expectation; registration/confirmation/enrolment outcome and assistance; return/Continue outcome; where help was needed; save/reopen outcome; context-selection explanation; checked result; perceived usefulness; approximate time; access barriers; suggested change; permission for any public quote. Store completed forms under ignored `trial/pilot/`. No real names/emails in public issues. Do not record passwords, AI chats or personal files without explicit need and consent.

## Public synthesis template

Number invited/participated/completed; what was observed; evidence limitations; recurring friction; decision; at most three next improvements; corresponding issue/commit; next check. Separate human findings, Finlay's own walkthrough and synthetic browser findings. Do not infer learning gain from page views or completion ticks.


## Release decision

Finlay reviews the real course experience, support/privacy wording and genuine confirmation/recovery receipt before broad enrolment. Check the complete journey on a phone and with keyboard navigation; include assistive-technology feedback where available and record any coverage limits. Fix blocking access, saving, recovery or usability problems before public launch. If fewer participants are available, record the actual count and retain limited-beta status rather than asserting the pilot passed. Select at most three further changes from the sanitised evidence. Do not publish invitations, learner quotes or LinkedIn posts on Finlay's behalf.
