## Evidence and limits

Screenshots from the public atlas are private local artifacts `/tmp/orientation-atlas-{1440,390,320}.png`. Raw native reports/captures stay in ignored fixture storage. The legacy broad public suite was not run: its configuration targets production and loads historical private QA credentials. Its stale landing/schema/version expectations were updated as source maintenance only.

The inspected `trial/launch-fixture/u9-fresh-learner-journey.png` shows the fictional learner's completed first activity, a separately explained Revisit suggestion and Continue to the next unfinished activity. The atlas/details and native journey/Help navigation remain visible. Test iteration corrected native-readiness waits and followed the actual post-logout home/account link rather than assuming logout opens the login form; those early failed attempts are not counted as passes. The separate delayed-probe regression established the cookie failure before changing product code. The final native report is `trial/launch-fixture/onboarding-report.json`; the initial nine-pass/one-failure receipt is retained privately as `u9-onboarding-first-run.json`.

Human first-impression review, consenting 3–5-person pilot, physical devices, assistive technologies, representative genuine mail delivery, the new saved-rating wording review and broader-launch retention remain outstanding. A synthetic readiness receipt is not owner approval. Existing genuine owner-controlled confirmation/recovery evidence is historical and separate; this rehearsal sends no real email.
