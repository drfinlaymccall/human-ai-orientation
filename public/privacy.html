About your information

This learning design project is run by Finlay McCall. The public course and portfolio builder can be used without an account.

If you use a pilot account

Moodle saves your account details, lesson progress, submitted reflection and activity logs on Finlay’s server. Finlay can see those records to support you and improve the course. This prototype uses essential login cookies. Cloudflare and the server also process web requests for delivery and security.

Keep your personal context portfolio and sensitive details in your own notes. Submit only a short, non-sensitive reflection. The course does not connect to your AI account or send your reflection to an AI provider. Information you independently share with your chosen AI is handled by that provider.

The interactive brief and practice workspace

The brief builder and try/check workspace keep your draft, prediction, pasted AI reply and checking notes temporarily in this browser tab using session storage. They are not submitted to Moodle, sent to an AI or saved to a course account by the builder. Copy work you want to keep into your own notes. Use Clear draft in the builder and Clear this try in the practice workspace to remove their temporary copies, especially on a shared device. Browser session restoration can retain a tab’s draft; closing a tab is not a guaranteed deletion method. If browser storage is unavailable, the builder tells you to copy before leaving.

Your portable portfolio

The portfolio builder keeps a temporary draft in this browser tab. Download the JSON backup to restore all your entries later; Markdown downloads are for reading and sharing selected context. The backup includes private practice notes: keep it private. Import reads a file on your device and does not upload it. Use “Clear this portfolio” to remove the temporary draft, and delete downloaded copies separately when you no longer need them. A browser may restore a closed tab, so closing it alone does not guarantee deletion.

Email and account access

Pilot accounts are provided directly by Finlay. Password recovery is available through Moodle’s Lost password link. Outgoing email is sent through Gmail using the course address hi@finlaymccall.com; incoming mail is forwarded through Cloudflare. Public registration and reminder emails remain disabled.

Optional voice and written feedback

When enabled for your course, private feedback is available to signed-in enrolled learners. Recording starts only after you choose Record and allow microphone access. You can listen, discard or record again before choosing Send. Audio stays in the page until you send it; the recorder does not save it in browser storage or copy your portfolio entries.

After Send, Moodle stores your feedback text, recording, account identifier, course/activity, optional section label and submission time on Finlay’s server. You and authorised site reviewers can access it. A generic notification tells the reviewer that feedback is waiting; it contains no recording or feedback text. This feature does not transcribe recordings or send them to an AI provider.

The default feedback retention is 90 days, with daily deletion through Moodle cron. Finlay will update this notice if that setting changes. Deleted files may remain in Moodle trash and server backups according to their separate schedules. Email hi@finlaymccall.com for a copy, deletion or a human-only response. You can always use text or email instead of recording.

Your records

Progress remains saved between visits. Email hi@finlaymccall.com to request a copy or deletion of your records or ask for help. Support requests are kept privately. An AI may help draft a reply using the minimum relevant request text; Finlay reviews the exact reply before sending. Ask for a human-only response if you prefer. Do not include your personal portfolio or passwords. The wider course and its data-retention arrangements are still being developed.
