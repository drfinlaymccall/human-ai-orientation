About your information

This learning design project is run by Finlay McCall. The public course and portfolio builder can be used without an account.

If you use a course account

Moodle saves your account details, lesson progress, submitted reflection and activity logs on Finlay’s server. Finlay can see those records to support you and improve the course. Moodle uses essential login cookies. Cloudflare and the server also process web requests for delivery and security.

Keep sensitive details out of course records and saved context. Submit only a short, non-sensitive reflection. The course does not connect to your AI account or send your reflection or saved context to an AI provider. Information you independently share with your chosen AI is handled by that provider.

The interactive brief and practice workspace

The brief builder and try/check workspace keep your draft, prediction, pasted AI reply and checking notes temporarily in this browser tab using session storage. Typing alone does not submit them to Moodle or an AI. Signed-in learners can deliberately choose “Save to My context” for the readable brief or try/check record; otherwise copy work you want to keep into your notes. Use Clear draft in the builder and Clear this try in the practice workspace to remove their temporary copies, especially on a shared device. Browser session restoration can retain a tab’s draft; closing a tab is not a guaranteed deletion method. If browser storage is unavailable, the builder tells you to copy before leaving.

Your context and portfolio

Editing the portfolio keeps a temporary draft in this browser tab. When signed in and enrolled, you can choose “Save to My context” to store the three context documents, review/example labels and separate private practice notes in your Moodle account on Finlay’s server. Saving a brief or try/check record also requires choosing Save. Moodle stores your text, title, source activity, account/course identifier, revision and creation/save times. Simply typing, copying, importing a backup or downloading does not save those changes to Moodle. Saved work returns when you sign in on another device.

My context lets you edit saved words, copy them and optionally download Markdown (.md). Other learners cannot access your workspace. Finlay administers the site and can access stored records for support and records requests; they are not published or automatically sent to an AI. Private practice notes are kept separate from selected AI-context copies. No automatic retention period has been set for context: it stays until you clear/delete it or request account/record deletion. The workspace offers confirmed note deletion and document clearing, and supports Moodle privacy export/deletion. Server backups may retain earlier copies under their separate schedules.

Anonymous use still keeps only a temporary browser draft. Download a private JSON backup to restore all fields, or copy selected context into your notes. Import reads a file on your device; only a later deliberate Save updates Moodle. Keep backups private because they include practice notes. “Clear this portfolio” clears the temporary draft; it does not clear previously saved Moodle work unless you then save the blank version. Use My context to remove saved text. Delete copied/downloaded files separately. Closing a tab alone does not guarantee deletion, since browsers may restore it.

Email and account access

Public registration is not open yet; accounts are provided directly by Finlay. When registration opens, an email link confirms access to the address supplied before you can use protected course activities. Account confirmation and password recovery use Moodle email links; never share those links or passwords. Use Recover access to request a reset or resend confirmation for an unconfirmed account. Outgoing email is sent through Gmail using the course address hi@finlaymccall.com; incoming mail is forwarded through Cloudflare. Reminder and marketing campaigns remain disabled; creating a course account does not subscribe you to them.

Your records

Progress remains saved between visits. Email hi@finlaymccall.com to request a copy or deletion of your records or ask for help. Support requests are kept privately. An AI may help draft a reply using the minimum relevant request text; Finlay reviews the exact reply before sending. Ask for a human-only response if you prefer. Do not include your personal portfolio or passwords. A fixed retention period has not been adopted. Finlay must review the retention and operating arrangements before broad enrolment opens. Contact Finlay about account access, deletion or how server backups affect a request; deleting your own downloaded copies remains your responsibility.
