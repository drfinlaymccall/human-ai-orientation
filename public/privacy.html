About your information

This learning design project is run by Finlay McCall. The public course and portfolio builder can be used without an account.

If you use a course account

Moodle saves your account details, lesson progress, submitted reflection and activity logs on Finlay’s server. Finlay can see those records to support you and improve the course. Moodle uses essential login cookies. Cloudflare and the server also process web requests for delivery and security.

Keep your personal context portfolio and sensitive details in your own notes. Submit only a short, non-sensitive reflection. The course does not connect to your AI account or send your reflection to an AI provider. Information you independently share with your chosen AI is handled by that provider.

Optional starting-point ratings

If you choose the optional starting-point check when it is enabled, Moodle saves your understanding and can-do responses and dated Quiz attempts, including unfinished work, on Finlay’s server. Not sure, skipped and unanswered responses remain unknown. Finlay can access these records for course support. Your private journey reads your latest finished compatible attempt to explain a practice suggestion; it does not infer proficiency or change activity completion. You can ignore the suggestion, skip the check or retake it. Retakes preserve earlier attempts; an earlier instrument remains dated history and is not reinterpreted. Ratings and suggestions are private to your signed-in journey and authorised course administration; they are not included in public course information or sent to an AI provider.

Contact Finlay for a copy or deletion of your saved ratings and attempts. The records and backups follow the account-record arrangements below; a fixed retention period has not yet been adopted. The new check remains unavailable to pilot/public learners until Finlay reviews this saved-rating wording.

The interactive brief and practice workspace

The brief builder and try/check workspace keep your draft, prediction, pasted AI reply and checking notes temporarily in this browser tab using session storage. They are not submitted to Moodle, sent to an AI or saved to a course account by the builder. Copy work you want to keep into your own notes. Use Clear draft in the builder and Clear this try in the practice workspace to remove their temporary copies, especially on a shared device. Browser session restoration can retain a tab’s draft; closing a tab is not a guaranteed deletion method. If browser storage is unavailable, the builder tells you to copy before leaving.

Your portable portfolio

The portfolio builder keeps a temporary draft in this browser tab. Download the JSON backup to restore all your entries later; Markdown downloads are for reading and sharing selected context. The backup includes private practice notes: keep it private. Import reads a file on your device and does not upload it. Use “Clear this portfolio” to remove the temporary draft, and delete downloaded copies separately when you no longer need them. A browser may restore a closed tab, so closing it alone does not guarantee deletion.

Email and account access

Public registration is not open yet; accounts are provided directly by Finlay. When registration opens, an email link confirms access to the address supplied before you can use protected course activities. Account confirmation and password recovery use Moodle email links; never share those links or passwords. Use Recover access to request a reset or resend confirmation for an unconfirmed account. Outgoing email is sent through Gmail using the course address hi@finlaymccall.com; incoming mail is forwarded through Cloudflare. Reminder and marketing campaigns remain disabled; creating a course account does not subscribe you to them.

Your records

Progress remains saved between visits. Email hi@finlaymccall.com to request a copy or deletion of your records or ask for help. Support requests are kept privately. An AI may help draft a reply using the minimum relevant request text; Finlay reviews the exact reply before sending. Ask for a human-only response if you prefer. Do not include your personal portfolio or passwords. A fixed retention period has not been adopted. Finlay must review the retention and operating arrangements before broad enrolment opens. Contact Finlay about account access, deletion or how server backups affect a request; deleting your own downloaded copies remains your responsibility.
