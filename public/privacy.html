Privacy policy

Human AI Orientation is run by Finlay McCall in Australia. This policy explains what personal information the course collects, why, and the choices you have. It follows the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Questions and requests go to Finlay at hi@finlaymccall.com.

Using the course without an account

You can read and practise with the public course without an account and without telling us who you are. We do not ask for your name or email to use it. Like most websites, the server and Cloudflare process each web request, including your IP address, to deliver the page and protect the site. Cloudflare also counts page views for us. This counting does not set a cookie.

You can use a fictional example for any activity. Please keep sensitive information, such as health details or other people’s private details, out of anything you save or send to the course.

What we collect and why

We collect information directly from you when you create an account or use the features below. We do not buy it from anyone else. We use it only to run the course, support you and improve the course. If you do not give the details a sign-up form needs, you cannot have an account, but you can still use the public course.

Finlay can see course records to support you and improve the course. Other learners cannot see your records or My context. We do not send your reflections, saved context, ratings or recordings to an AI provider, and the course does not connect to your AI account. What you paste into your own AI tool is handled by that tool’s provider under its terms.

The starting-point suggestion is worked out automatically from your own ratings. It only suggests where you might practise. It does not affect your completion, access or any decision about you.

Moodle sets one essential session cookie when you visit. If you have an account, it keeps you signed in. The course does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We do not send marketing email. Creating an account does not subscribe you to anything, and we will ask first if that ever changes.

Course accounts are for people aged 18 or over. The public course needs no account.

What stays in your browser

The brief builder, try/check workspace and portfolio keep a temporary draft in your browser tab. This stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you choose to save it. Use Clear draft, Clear this try or Clear this portfolio to remove it, especially on a shared device. Closing a tab may not remove it, because browsers can restore tabs.

Portfolio backups and Markdown downloads are files on your device. We cannot see or delete them, so keep them private.

Who else handles it

We do not sell personal information. These services handle some of it for us, and some are located or operate overseas:

  • Contabo hosts the course server, where course records are stored. Network records place this server in New South Wales, Australia. Contabo also provides server backups.
  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the site and forwards incoming email. It processes web requests and forwarded email through a global network, including in the United States.
  • Google (Gmail) sends course email, such as account confirmation and password recovery, and support replies. Those messages are stored and processed by Google, including in the United States.
  • An AI service may help Finlay draft a support reply, using only the relevant part of your request. Finlay uses Anthropic and OpenAI services, which operate from the United States. Finlay reviews every reply before it is sent. Ask for a human-only reply if you prefer; your support will not change.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

  • Feedback: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Accounts and course records, including saved context and ratings: kept until you delete them or ask us to, and reviewed once a year. We delete accounts that have been inactive for 24 months.
  • Support emails: kept for 24 months, then deleted.
  • Backups: deleted information can stay in server backups or Moodle’s trash until those copies are overwritten.

We protect records with encrypted connections, restricted administrator access and owner-only access to saved work. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Getting a copy, correcting or deleting

You can edit, copy, download and delete your saved context in My context. Email hi@finlaymccall.com to ask for a copy of your records, a correction or deletion of your account, or to ask what we hold about you. It is free. We will reply within 30 days. To protect you, we may first confirm that the request comes from your account’s email address. Please do not send passwords, reset links or private backups.

Making a complaint

Email hi@finlaymccall.com with what happened and what you would like us to do. Finlay will look into it and reply in writing within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied, or do not hear back within 30 days, you can complain to the OAIC. Call 1300 363 992, email oaicintake@oaic.gov.au, or write to GPO Box 5288, Sydney NSW 2001.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our practices change, and tell account holders by email about any significant change.
