Human AI Orientation · Free public activities

Explore your course journey.

Start with a useful task. Learn what context helps, then build a small portfolio you can find, review and reuse in another conversation.

Work at your own pace. Allow roughly 70–95 minutes for the three activities and portfolio bridge, split across visits if useful. Times are guides, not deadlines. A free AI tool is enough; fictional examples let you practise without an AI account.

What would you like to do with AI?

Three core activities, with portfolio practice inside the context activity and optional preparation. Bring your own task or use a fictional example. You can begin anywhere and return to any activity.

Core activity · 10–15 minutes

Try AI on a useful task

Open this activity

You'll do

Choose something that matters, decide what a useful result would look like and make a first attempt.

You'll keep

A first result and one clear criterion you can use to judge it.

What you'll practise

  • Choosing a worthwhile task.
  • Explaining what you want.
  • Judging what help you need.
Check what I can do

What I understand

Can I explain why this task matters and what a useful result would look like?

What I can do

Can I choose a task, give an instruction and compare the result with my criterion?

Use these questions for your own reflection. No answer or assessment is saved here.

Core activity · 25–30 minutes

Give AI the right background

Open this activity

You'll do

Choose helpful details, write a short brief, try it with your AI and work through anything it gets wrong about your intention.

You'll keep

A brief you can reuse in another conversation.

What you'll practise

  • Selecting relevant information and protecting private details.
  • Explaining your intention.
  • Identifying a misunderstanding, clarifying it and checking the revision.
Check what I can do

What I understand

Can I explain why a detail helps, what I leave out and how a clarification could address a mismatch?

What I can do

Can I select useful context, identify a mismatch, clarify it and check the revision?

Portfolio bridge · Part of activity 2 · 20–30 minutes

Save your context for next time

Open this activity

You'll do

Organise useful information about yourself, a project and your sources. Review it, save your documents and reopen them.

You'll keep

Your own reviewed context portfolio, ready to find and selectively reuse.

What you'll practise

  • Organising useful information.
  • Reviewing what to keep and share.
  • Saving, finding and reopening documents.
Check what I can do

What I understand

Can I explain what belongs in my personal, project and source notes, and how I will find them again?

What I can do

Can I make a reviewed context document, save it outside this page and reopen the intended copy?

Core activity · 15–20 minutes

Check a result and plan your next step

Open this activity

You'll do

Use an existing brief or an example in a fresh task. Check a result, correct a problem and choose a meaningful next goal.

You'll keep

A checked result, an improvement worth keeping and a manageable next-step plan.

What you'll practise

  • Checking a result against a criterion.
  • Making and evaluating a correction.
  • Reusing context and choosing further practice.
Check what I can do

What I understand

Can I explain how I checked the result and distinguish my learning needs from information my AI needs?

What I can do

Can I test a result, evaluate a correction and design a first step towards my next goal?

Optional preparation · 20–30 minutes

Files, notes and AI basics — optional help

Open this activity

You'll do

Practise saving and finding a file, keeping a useful note and using an available way to give your AI an instruction.

You'll keep

A file and note you can find again, plus a way to give your AI an instruction.

What you'll practise

  • Saving and locating files.
  • Creating and reopening a useful note.
  • Transferring information and checking what arrived.
Check what I can do

What I understand

Can I explain where my file is stored and how I can return to it?

What I can do

Can I save, locate and reopen a file, and transfer a useful excerpt into a conversation?
