Finlay’s tip · Take one useful detour

Want a quick explanation? Start with Code.org’s chatbot video. Want to practise collaborating and judging results? Try AI Fluency. Want the bigger picture? Choose Elements of AI or the Open University’s introduction. Bring one idea back and try it on your own task.

The fit descriptions below are Finlay’s course-design recommendations, based on the providers’ published outlines. They are not rankings or a claim that we have completed every course. Links open a new tab.

Bring something back to your own task

  1. Before leaving: choose one question, such as “How can I make my instruction clearer?”
  2. After a short section: explain one useful idea in your own words.
  3. Try it: use that idea in an AI conversation and check what changed. Keep the resource link and your observation in your notes.
Show me an example reflection

Fictional example: “I wanted to make my instruction clearer. I learned to describe the result I need. I asked for three checklist items instead of general advice, and checked that each item was something I could actually do. I still need to confirm the suggested timing.”

External courses keep their own accounts and progress. Finishing one does not automatically mark work complete here. You can return to the context lesson whenever you are ready.
