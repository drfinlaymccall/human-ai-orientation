Course and account help

Get back to your next useful step.

You can read and practise with the public course without an account. Public registration is not open yet. Existing and invited learners can sign in to keep Moodle activity progress and deliberately save their creations in My context.

Sign in, confirm or recover an account

  • Sign in with your existing username and password. Already registered? Recover that account rather than make another.
  • Recover access or resend confirmation: enter your username or account email, not both. For an unconfirmed account, Moodle sends confirmation again; for a confirmed account it sends a password-reset link. Its on-screen response deliberately does not reveal whether an account exists. If registration is paused, confirmation is paused too: contact Finlay about an unconfirmed account.
  • Check spam and the spelling of your account email. Open the email link in any browser; use the newest message. If a reset link has expired or was already used, request another. A confirmation link used twice says the account is already confirmed: return to sign-in.
  • After confirmation, open your intended course activity. If Moodle asks, choose Enrol me. Existing enrolled learners do not need to enrol again. If you opened confirmation on a different device, return to the activity you wanted, or use the course link on the sign-in page.
  • If no email arrives or the address needs correcting, contact Finlay. Never send the confirmation/reset link or your password.

Account-confirmation emails activate a new account; password-recovery emails let you set a new password. If a recovery link says “You are already logged in”, sign out first or open the email link in a private/incognito window. That message describes the current browser session; it does not confirm another account.

Keep and return to your work

Moodle saves activity completion and submitted lesson reflections. Simply opening a Page does not complete its practice: use its completion control when you have done it. In your portfolio, editing keeps a temporary tab draft; choose Save to My context to keep it in Moodle. Return through My context to edit saved documents, use the big Copy button, optionally download a Markdown (.md) file, or jump back to the activity. Saved work is available on another signed-in device. Anonymous drafts still need a private JSON backup to restore later; keep backups private. Importing a backup does not automatically update Moodle.

Ask Finlay for help

Email hi@finlaymccall.com. Include the course page, what you wanted to do, what happened, what you tried and your device/browser if relevant. A fictional example or redacted screenshot is enough. Do not include passwords, reset links, private portfolio backups or sensitive personal information.

Finlay reviews support replies before sending; an AI may help draft a reply using the minimum relevant request text. You can ask for a human-only reply. This is a small course with no guaranteed response time. See about your information, or give your AI the public course guide.

Give Finlay feedback by voice or text

Signed-in course learners can leave private voice or written feedback. Record a short message, listen back, and choose Send when you are ready. On a Moodle activity, use its feedback link to include the activity automatically.

Name the part you mean, explain what helped or got in the way, and suggest what would make it clearer. Text and audio-file upload are available too. Without a course account, use the email address above.
