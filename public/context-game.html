My context

Unit 1 · Optional quiz · About 5 minutes

Pack the brief.

Finish Unit 1 with a short quiz. It has four stages, one for each step of the unit. Each stage says what it practises or checks, then uses one example: Mina’s party invitation.

Why this helps: you use the four steps together on one small task, and you see how each choice changes the AI’s reply.

Mina has moved into a new flat and is having a housewarming party. She wants an AI to draft the invitation email to her friends. Mina is fictional, and so is every AI reply in this quiz.

Your choices stay on this page. Nothing is saved or sent, and no AI is contacted.

Do the quiz on paper

The interactive quiz needs JavaScript. This is the same quiz. Each stage says which skill from this unit it practises or checks. Answer each question in your head or in your notes, then open the answer.

1. Set the criterion

What this stage checks: whether you can tell a criterion you can check from one you cannot. You decide what a useful result looks like before you ask, so that you can judge the reply. From Step 1.

Before Mina asks the AI, she decides what a useful invitation looks like. Which of these can she check when the reply comes back?

  1. It should be really good.
  2. Under 80 words, friendly, gives the date and time, and says what to bring.
  3. It should sound like something I would write.
Answer

“Under 80 words, friendly, gives the date and time, and says what to bring.” Mina can count the words, and she can look for the date, the time and what to bring.

2. Choose the context

What this stage practises: choosing context. You give an AI what helps with this task, and you leave out what is unrelated or private. From Step 2.

These are 8 of Mina’s notes. Which would you give the AI to write this invitation?

  • The email goes to my close friends. Most of them know each other.
  • The party is on 21 June from 6 pm at my new flat. Leave a placeholder for the address. I will add it myself.
  • People should bring a drink to share. I am providing the food.
  • Keep it plain and friendly. No shouting.
  • I also need to book a dentist appointment.
  • My laptop battery does not last long.
  • My friend Priya has just lost her job, so she may not be able to bring anything.
  • My address is Flat 5, 22 Wattle Street. The door code is 4471.
Answer

Helps with this task:

  • The email goes to my close friends. Most of them know each other.
  • The party is on 21 June from 6 pm at my new flat. Leave a placeholder for the address. I will add it myself.
  • People should bring a drink to share. I am providing the food.
  • Keep it plain and friendly. No shouting.

Not about this task:

  • I also need to book a dentist appointment.
  • My laptop battery does not last long.

Private: leave it out:

  • My friend Priya has just lost her job, so she may not be able to bring anything.
  • My address is Flat 5, 22 Wattle Street. The door code is 4471.

3. Check the reply

What this stage practises: checking a reply against your criterion and your own notes, then correcting it with a specific instruction. From Step 4.

Suppose Mina gave the AI only these notes:

  • The email goes to my close friends. Most of them know each other.
  • I also need to book a dentist appointment.
  • My address is Flat 5, 22 Wattle Street. The door code is 4471.

The AI replied:

Hi everyone, I have moved, and I would love you to come to my housewarming. It is this Saturday at 7 pm. Please bring a plate of food and a gift. It is going to be the PARTY OF THE YEAR!!! There is plenty of parking on the street. P.S. Can anyone recommend a dentist? The address is Flat 5, 22 Wattle Street, and the door code is 4471.

Mina’s criterion: Under 80 words, friendly, gives the date and time, and says what to bring. Find 6 sentences she should not send as they are.

Answer
  • “It is this Saturday at 7 pm.” The AI was not told the date or the time, so it made them up.
  • “Please bring a plate of food and a gift.” The AI guessed what to bring. Mina’s note says a drink to share, and that she is providing the food.
  • “It is going to be the PARTY OF THE YEAR!!!” The AI was not told how the email should sound, so it chose for itself.
  • “There is plenty of parking on the street.” None of Mina’s notes mentions parking. An AI can add a detail that sounds right even when the context is good. That is why you check.
  • “P.S. Can anyone recommend a dentist?” The dentist note had nothing to do with this task, and the AI used it anyway.
  • “The address is Flat 5, 22 Wattle Street, and the door code is 4471.” Mina planned to add the address herself, and the AI never needed the door code. Now both are in a group email.

The parking sentence is one of them. What should Mina say to the AI?

  1. Make it better.
  2. You said there is plenty of parking. I did not say that. Remove it, and do not add details I have not given you.
  3. Say nothing. Ask the same question again in a new conversation.
Answer

“You said there is plenty of parking. I did not say that. Remove it, and do not add details I have not given you.” Mina names the problem, says what to change and gives a rule that will help next time.

4. Keep what worked

What this stage checks: whether you know what belongs in each of your three documents, and what to keep out of them. From Step 3.

Mina keeps three documents she can use again with any AI:

  • Working with me: how Mina wants an AI to work with her on any task.
  • My goals and projects: what she is working towards, and the next step.
  • Useful knowledge and examples: facts and examples worth using again.

Where does each note go, and which one does she leave out?

  • Do not add details I have not given you. Ask me instead.
  • Goal: housewarming party on 21 June. Next step: send the invitation this week.
  • An invitation that worked: short and friendly, with the date and time first and what to bring last.
  • The door code for my building is 4471.
Answer
  • “Do not add details I have not given you. Ask me instead.” Working with me. This is how Mina wants an AI to work with her on any task.
  • “Goal: housewarming party on 21 June. Next step: send the invitation this week.” My goals and projects. This is a goal and its next step.
  • “An invitation that worked: short and friendly, with the date and time first and what to bring last.” Useful knowledge and examples. This is an example of what worked. Mina can use it again for her next invitation.
  • “The door code for my building is 4471.” Leave it out. Private details do not belong in notes Mina will paste into an AI conversation.

What the quiz covered

  • Step 1: Decide what a useful result looks like before you ask.
  • Step 2: Give the AI what helps with this task. Leave out what is unrelated or private.
  • Step 4: Check the result against your criterion, and correct it with a specific instruction.
  • Step 3: Keep what worked in your three documents, so you can use it again.
