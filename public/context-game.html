My context

Unit 1 · Optional game · About 5 minutes

Pack the brief.

Finish Unit 1 with a short game. Help Mina get a useful notice from an AI: set a criterion, choose the context, check the reply and keep what worked.

Why this helps: the game puts the four steps of this unit into one small task, so you can see how each choice changes the result.

Mina helps at a community garden on Wattle Street. She wants an AI to draft a short notice that invites her neighbours to a garden tidy-up morning. Mina is fictional, and so is every AI reply in this game.

Your choices stay on this page. Nothing is saved or sent, and no AI is contacted.

Play on paper

The interactive game needs JavaScript. This is the same game. Answer each question in your head or in your notes, then open the answer.

1. Set the criterion

Before Mina asks, she decides what a useful notice looks like. Which of these can she check?

  1. It should be really good.
  2. Under 80 words, friendly, says what to bring, and leaves a placeholder for the date, which is not decided.
  3. It should sound like something I would write.
Answer

“Under 80 words, friendly, says what to bring, and leaves a placeholder for the date, which is not decided.” Mina can count the words, look for what to bring and look for the placeholder.

2. Choose the context

These are 8 of Mina’s notes. Which would you give the AI for this task?

  • The notice is for neighbours on Wattle Street. Many have not met each other.
  • People should bring gloves and a water bottle. Tools are provided.
  • The date is not decided. Leave a placeholder.
  • Keep it plain and friendly. No shouting.
  • I am also planning my sister’s birthday in March.
  • My printer is low on ink.
  • Mrs Alvarez at number 12 has been unwell and cannot lift things.
  • The code for the shed key safe is 4471.
Answer

Helps with this task:

  • The notice is for neighbours on Wattle Street. Many have not met each other.
  • People should bring gloves and a water bottle. Tools are provided.
  • The date is not decided. Leave a placeholder.
  • Keep it plain and friendly. No shouting.

Not about this task:

  • I am also planning my sister’s birthday in March.
  • My printer is low on ink.

Private: leave it out:

  • Mrs Alvarez at number 12 has been unwell and cannot lift things.
  • The code for the shed key safe is 4471.

3. Check the reply

Suppose Mina gave the AI only these notes:

  • The notice is for neighbours on Wattle Street. Many have not met each other.
  • I am also planning my sister’s birthday in March.
  • The code for the shed key safe is 4471.

The AI replied:

Hello, Wattle Street neighbours. Come and meet the people next door at our garden tidy-up morning. See you this Saturday at 9 am. Bring your own spade and wheelbarrow. It is going to be ABSOLUTELY AMAZING!!! Morning tea will be provided. P.S. Happy birthday in March to Mina’s sister. The code for the shed key safe is 4471.

Mina’s criterion: Under 80 words, friendly, says what to bring, and leaves a placeholder for the date. Find 6 sentences she should not send as they are.

Answer
  • “See you this Saturday at 9 am.” The AI was not told the date is undecided, so it made one up.
  • “Bring your own spade and wheelbarrow.” The AI guessed what to bring. Mina’s note says gloves and a water bottle, and that tools are provided.
  • “It is going to be ABSOLUTELY AMAZING!!!” The AI was not told how the notice should sound, so it chose for itself.
  • “Morning tea will be provided.” None of Mina’s notes mentions morning tea. An AI can add a detail that sounds right even when the context is good. That is why you check.
  • “P.S. Happy birthday in March to Mina’s sister.” The birthday note had nothing to do with this task, and the AI used it anyway.
  • “The code for the shed key safe is 4471.” The code was private. The AI did not need it, and now it is in a public notice.

Morning tea is one of them. What should Mina say to the AI?

  1. Make it better.
  2. You added morning tea. I did not say that. Remove it, and do not add arrangements I have not given you.
  3. Say nothing. Ask the same question again in a new conversation.
Answer

“You added morning tea. I did not say that. Remove it, and do not add arrangements I have not given you.” Mina names the problem, says what to change and gives a rule that will help next time.

4. Keep what worked

Mina keeps three documents: Working with me, My goals and projects, Useful knowledge and examples. Where does each note go, and which one does she leave out?

  • Do not add arrangements I have not given you. Ask me instead.
  • Goal: 15 neighbours at the garden tidy-up. Next step: agree the date with the garden committee.
  • What to bring: gloves and a water bottle; tools are provided. From the garden committee’s list, checked on 3 May.
  • The code for the shed key safe is 4471.
Answer
  • “Do not add arrangements I have not given you. Ask me instead.” Working with me. This is how Mina wants an AI to work with her on any task.
  • “Goal: 15 neighbours at the garden tidy-up. Next step: agree the date with the garden committee.” My goals and projects. This is a goal and its next step.
  • “What to bring: gloves and a water bottle; tools are provided. From the garden committee’s list, checked on 3 May.” Useful knowledge and examples. This is a fact with its source and the date Mina checked it.
  • “The code for the shed key safe is 4471.” Leave it out. Private details do not belong in notes Mina will paste into an AI conversation.

What the game shows

  • Step 1: Decide what a useful result looks like before you ask.
  • Step 2: Give the AI what helps with this task. Leave out what is unrelated or private.
  • Step 4: Check the result against your criterion, and correct it with a specific instruction.
  • Step 3: Keep what worked in your three documents, so you can use it again.
