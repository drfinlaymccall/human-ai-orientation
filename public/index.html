Human AI Orientation

Make a useful start with AI.

Turn a task that matters to you into a better AI result. Learn what context to give, what to check, and what to keep for next time.

For people who use everyday online tools and want to work more purposefully with AI. No AI expertise or coding experience is needed.

Explore your course

70–95 minutes, at your own pace · No course account needed to start · Free AI tools are enough

Prefer to explore first? Every activity includes a fictional example, so you can practise without an AI account.

Something useful to take away

Your own context portfolio.

Three short documents you can review, download and reuse:

  • Working with me: useful preferences and boundaries.
  • My goals and projects: the task, context and next step.
  • Useful knowledge and examples: information worth bringing along.

Test the relevant parts in a fresh conversation. Leave with a checked result and a goal you can act on.

The full public course and portfolio builder are available without signing in. Public registration is currently closed. Accounts are currently provided by Finlay for the pilot; existing pilot learners can sign in to their saved Moodle activity progress and submitted reflection. Ask for help if you need access support.

Your portfolio stays separate from your account. Browser drafts are temporary: download your context documents and a JSON backup before leaving, then restore that backup when you return or change devices. Without JavaScript, copy the plain-text templates into your own notes.

Bring your AI into the course

Give your AI the public course context, then check what it read. If it cannot open a link, paste the same text instead. No GitHub account or AI browsing feature is required.
