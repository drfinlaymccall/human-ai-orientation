Make a useful start with AI.

Find your way around files and notes, then explore typing, speaking and sharing files or pictures with your AI. Choose something that matters to you, give it helpful context, and learn to judge the result.

Bring your AI into the lesson

Give your AI the course context, then check what it read. If it cannot open a link, paste the same text instead. No GitHub account or AI browsing feature is required.

A working course, growing through practice.

You can use the full course journey and portfolio builder without an account. Pilot accounts save your place and a short reflection in Moodle. Accounts are currently provided by Finlay; password recovery works by email. Public sign-up and reminders are still being developed.

Download your personal context portfolio and a JSON backup to restore it later. Browser drafts are temporary; Moodle stores progress separately. If you use a pilot account, share only a brief, non-sensitive reflection.
