Make a useful start with AI.

Find your way around files and notes, then explore typing, speaking and sharing files or pictures with your AI. Choose something that matters to you, give it helpful context, and learn to judge the result.

Bring your AI into the lesson

Give your AI the course context, then check what it read. If it cannot open a link, paste the same text instead. No GitHub account or AI browsing feature is required.

This is the first working prototype.

You can use the files-and-folders activity, notes guide and whole AI lesson without an account. Pilot accounts save your place and a short reflection in Moodle. Accounts are currently provided by Finlay; public sign-up and email reminders are still being developed.

Keep your personal context portfolio in your own notes. If you use a pilot account, share only a brief, non-sensitive reflection.
