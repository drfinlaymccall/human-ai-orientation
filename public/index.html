Human AI Orientation

Learn to team with AI.

Choose a useful task, give context, check the result and keep what helps. Practise communicating what you need and working through misunderstandings.

For people who use everyday online tools and want to work more purposefully with AI. No AI expertise or coding experience is needed.

70–95 minutes, at your own pace · No course account needed to start · Free AI tools are enough

Prefer to explore first? Every activity includes a fictional example, so you can practise without an AI account.

Something useful to take away

Your own context portfolio.

Three short documents you can review, download and reuse:

  • Working with me: useful preferences and boundaries.
  • My goals and projects: the task, context and next step.
  • Useful knowledge and examples: information worth bringing along.

Test them in a fresh conversation. Leave with a checked result and one goal you want to complete with AI’s help.

Four steps, in order

  1. Try AI on a useful task. Pick something small that matters to you and decide what a useful result looks like. 10–15 minutes.
  2. Give AI useful context. Tell the AI what it needs to know, then fix one thing it gets wrong. 25–30 minutes.
  3. Save and reuse context. Write your three context documents and keep them in your own notes. 20–30 minutes.
  4. Check, improve and continue. Test your documents in a fresh conversation and choose your next goal. 15–20 minutes.

You finish with three documents you can reuse with any AI, and one goal you want to complete with its help. Split the steps across visits if that suits you.

What you'll practise

By the end, you will have practised how to:

  • Choose a worthwhile task and decide what a useful result would look like.
  • Explain your intention, notice a misunderstanding and clarify it.
  • Choose relevant context, leave out unnecessary private details and check what the AI actually received.
  • Check a result against your purpose and decide what to keep, correct or reject.
  • Save, find and selectively reuse reviewed context in another conversation.
  • Identify your own next learning need and choose a manageable next step.

These are practice goals, not a graded assessment. Choose any activity and return when useful. This unit is useful on its own. Unit 2 is a planned syllabus, not an available set of activities.

Keep your work

Copy your work into your own notes as you go. That copy is yours, works with any AI and needs no course account. Step 3 shows you how.

The whole course is open without signing in. Public registration is currently closed. Accounts are currently provided by Finlay. If you have one, sign in to save your documents and progress in the course and reach them from another device. Ask for help if you need access support.

Bring your AI into the course

Give your AI the public course context, then check what it read. If it cannot open a link, paste the same text instead. No GitHub account or AI browsing feature is required.
