Why this unit?

This unit prepares you to collaborate with AI on tasks that matter to you, using whichever AI tool you choose. Practise explaining what you want, checking the result and deciding what to keep or change.

You control the context you give AI: the background, preferences and instructions it needs for your task. Keep your own reviewed notes and choose what to share in each conversation. Check what the AI received. This helps you maintain and reuse useful context across tools and apps.

The modules cover your everyday workspace: files, notes, conversations and ways to give AI information. The unit focuses on desktop and laptop use. Many ideas also apply to phones and tablets. Practice for familiar skills is optional.
