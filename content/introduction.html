Why this course?

This course prepares you to collaborate with AI on tasks that are meaningful to you, using whichever AI tool you choose. You practise explaining what you want, checking the result and deciding what to keep or change.

You stay in control of the context you give AI: the background, preferences and instructions it needs for your task. Keep your own reviewed notes, choose what to share in each conversation, and check what the AI has received. This helps you maintain and reuse useful context across tools and apps.

The course also helps you become familiar with the everyday working environment: files, notes, conversations and ways to give AI information. It is designed for desktop and laptop users, with optional preparation if you need it. Many of the ideas transfer to phones and tablets.

Work at your own pace. Allow roughly 70–95 minutes for the three activities and portfolio practice, split across visits if useful. A free AI tool is enough, fictional examples are available, and files-and-notes preparation is optional.

Your course journey is the activity map. Use it to choose where to begin, see your progress and return to your work.
