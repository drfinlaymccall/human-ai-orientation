Unit 1: Human–AI Orientation

Why this unit?

This unit prepares you to collaborate with AI on tasks that matter to you, using whichever AI tool you choose. Practise explaining what you want, checking the result and deciding what to keep or change.

You control the context you give AI: the background, preferences and instructions it needs for your task. Keep your own reviewed notes and choose what to share in each conversation. Check what the AI received. This helps you maintain and reuse useful context across tools and apps.

The modules cover your everyday workspace: files, notes, conversations and ways to give AI information. The unit focuses on desktop and laptop use. Many ideas also apply to phones and tablets. Practice for familiar skills is optional.

Learn to team with AI: choose a useful task, give context, check the result and keep what helps.

Work at your own pace. Allow roughly 70–95 minutes for the three activities and portfolio bridge, split across visits if useful. Times are guides, not deadlines. A free AI tool is enough; fictional examples let you practise without an AI account.

What you'll practise

By the end, you will have practised how to:

  • Choose a worthwhile task and decide what a useful result would look like.
  • Explain your intention, notice a misunderstanding and clarify it.
  • Choose relevant context, leave out unnecessary private details and check what the AI actually received.
  • Check a result against your purpose and decide what to keep, correct or reject.
  • Save, find and selectively reuse reviewed context in another conversation.
  • Identify your own next learning need and choose a manageable next step.

These are practice goals, not a graded assessment. Choose any activity and return when useful. This unit is useful on its own. Unit 2 is a planned syllabus, not an available set of activities.

The optional starting-point check asks about your understanding and what you can do. Sign in with an invited course account to save your ratings and see explained suggestions. You can choose any public activity without an account. Public registration is currently closed.

Unit 1 syllabus

Use your own AI for a useful task. Keep context you can review and reuse.

  1. Desktop AI workspace: An available way to give your AI an instruction.
  2. Files, folders and locations: Practise saving and finding a file, reading its location and sharing a useful excerpt.
  3. Notes as working context: A home for context notes you can review and reuse.
  4. Try AI on a useful task: Choose something that matters, decide what a useful result would look like and make a first attempt.
  5. Give AI useful context: Choose helpful details, write a short brief, try it with your AI and work through anything it gets wrong about your intention.
  6. Save and reuse context: Organise useful information about yourself, a project and your sources. Review it, save your documents and reopen them.
  7. Check, improve and continue: Use an existing brief or an example in a fresh task. Check a result, correct a problem and choose a meaningful next goal.

Returning to your work?

Signed-in learners can open My context to edit and copy work they deliberately saved to Moodle, including on another device. Your Unit 1 progress keeps activity completion separate from your documents. In anonymous use, open the portfolio builder in the same tab to resume its temporary draft, or restore your private JSON backup.

The outcome is practical: retrieve your reviewed documents, select relevant context and use it to begin a worthwhile task.
