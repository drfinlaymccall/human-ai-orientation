Your course journey · Free public activities

Build something you can use again.

Start with a useful task. Learn what context helps, then build a small portfolio you can find, review and reuse in another conversation.

Work at your own pace. Allow roughly 70–95 minutes for the three activities and portfolio bridge, split across visits if useful. Times are guides, not deadlines. A free AI tool is enough; fictional examples let you practise without an AI account.

  1. Choose and try

    Get one useful result · 10–15 minutes. Keep a task, a checkable criterion and your first attempt.

  2. Give useful context

    Choose the details that help · 25–30 minutes. Try a better brief, notice what changed and keep a reusable instruction.

    Portfolio bridge · Part of activity 2

    Make three small context documents · 20–30 minutes. Write them yourself or use an AI interview, review the text and download your own copy.

  3. Check and continue

    Test your portfolio in a fresh conversation · 15–20 minutes. Check a result, improve an instruction and choose a next goal.

Optional preparation

Find your way around files, notes and your AI · 20–30 minutes, in addition to the core journey. Practise saving and retrieving a file if you need it, or go straight to activity 1.

Returning to your work?

Open the portfolio builder in the same tab to resume its temporary draft, or restore your downloaded JSON backup. Public activities do not save progress to an account. Existing pilot learners can sign in for Moodle activity progress; your private portfolio remains separate.

The outcome is practical: retrieve your reviewed documents, select relevant context and use it to begin a worthwhile task.
