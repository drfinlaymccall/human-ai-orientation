Learn to collaborate with AI on tasks that matter to you. Choose what background to share, check how the AI uses it, and keep context you can update and reuse across tools and apps. Read the course rationale.

The course is designed for using AI on a desktop or laptop computer. Many of the ideas also apply on phones and tablets. Use whichever AI tool you choose; a free tool is enough.

Your course at a glance

Three practical activities, with portfolio practice as part of the context activity, help you learn to:

  • Files, notes and AI basics — optional help (optional preparation): Practise saving and finding a file, keeping a useful note and using an available way to give your AI an instruction.
  • Try AI on a useful task: Choose something that matters, decide what a useful result would look like and make a first attempt.
  • Give AI the right background: Choose helpful details, write a short brief, try it with your AI and work through anything it gets wrong about your intention.
  • Save your context for next time (part of activity 2): Organise useful information about yourself, a project and your sources. Review it, save your documents and reopen them.
  • Check a result and plan your next step: Use an existing brief or an example in a fresh task. Check a result, correct a problem and choose a meaningful next goal.

Leave with useful work, a reviewed collection of context notes you control, and a meaningful next goal. Allow roughly 70–95 minutes for the activities and portfolio practice, plus optional preparation. Work at your own pace, start where useful and return when you need to. Fictional examples are available.
