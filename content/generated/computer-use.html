In this first module, learn about files, notes and AI basics, and set up a note-taking app as your own context store: a collection of useful background, instructions and checked results that you maintain.

This supports the course rationale: you choose and manage the context you give AI. Keeping your own notes lets you move between AI apps as your needs change and the tools evolve. Practise finding your notes, updating them and copying only the relevant parts into whichever AI you choose.

Start with the files and AI basics, then set up your notes with Obsidian or an app you already use. If these skills are familiar, check that you can find, reopen and reuse a note, then continue to a useful AI task.
