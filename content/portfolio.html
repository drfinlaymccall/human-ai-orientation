Activity 2 extension · Build and keep · About 20–30 minutes

A little context you can reuse.

Turn what you have learned into three small documents: working with me, my goals and projects, and useful knowledge and examples. Start with a few lines. Leave anything out that you do not want to share.

Bring a useful instruction from the context activity. You can write about everyday life, study, a hobby or community work. No job title, diagnosis, full name or intimate personal history is required.

An interview prompt to copy

Select and copy the text below. Sending it to your AI starts a conversation in that service; this course does not connect to it.

I am building a small personal context portfolio for a task I choose. Help me draft three short documents: Working with me; My goals and projects; Useful knowledge and examples.
Ask one focused question at a time. First ask what small task I want help with. Let me skip any question. Do not ask for sensitive personal details or information about other people. Do not infer traits, diagnoses or facts I have not supplied.
After each section, show a short draft using only my answers. Ask me to correct or remove anything before continuing. Separate facts from guesses, and mark unknowns. Keep the wording plain and useful for the chosen task.
At the end, show the three reviewed drafts separately. Remind me to check them again, save my own copy and share only relevant parts in future conversations. Do not claim that you have saved them or will remember them.

With JavaScript enabled, entries can be kept temporarily in this browser tab. They are not saved to your Moodle account or sent to an AI. Download a backup before closing the tab or changing devices.

Review, then choose what to share

Read your documents. Correct invented or outdated details, remove anything unnecessary and mark unknowns. Checking this box records your review; it does not establish that an AI’s output is correct. Editing context clears the check.

Choose context for your next task

Start with one document. Add another only if it helps. Your practice record is never included here.

Keep it somewhere you can find again

  1. Download the context documents to read or share, and the backup if you want to restore the builder later.
  2. Find the downloaded files in your Downloads folder. Move them to your own notes folder if that helps.
  3. Open the Markdown file in a text editor or notes app. Check that it contains what you meant to keep.
  4. On a later visit, choose “Restore a portfolio backup” and select your JSON backup. Review before replacing any current entries.

Markdown is ordinary text with headings. A JSON backup is for restoring this builder; do not paste the whole backup into an AI. It includes your practice record. The course does not automatically update either file after download.
